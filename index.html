Where do I find this?
  1. Sign in to the Google account that owns the business profile.
  2. Search for your business name on Google. Your profile controls appear above the results.
  3. Choose Ask for reviews (the name of this button can vary) and copy the link it shows.
  4. Paste the link in the box above.

No access to the profile? Look up the business in Google's Place ID Finder and paste the Place ID instead.
